Safety

HR guard: auto step-down
When HR stays at or above the threshold for the set time, speed drops by the set amount for the rest of that step. This follows the plan's step-down rule. It needs an HR source: a chest strap, or an Apple Watch app that broadcasts HR (e.g. HeartCast).
